Interior permanent magnet synchronous motors (IPMSM) exploit both magnet torque and
reluctance torque, so the magnet arrangement and flux-barrier geometry inside the rotor
largely determine the achievable performance.

## Research topics

- Comparison of single- and multi-layer magnet layouts (V-type, delta-type, spoke-type) in terms of torque and back-EMF
- Reluctance torque and torque ripple as a function of flux-barrier shape
- Inductance design for an extended flux-weakening operating region
- Reduced rare-earth and ferrite-substituted designs

## Applications

Traction motors for electric vehicles, industrial servo drives, and home-appliance compressors,
where high efficiency and high power density are required.
