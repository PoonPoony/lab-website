Machine design is a classic multi-objective problem: efficiency, torque ripple, magnet usage and
temperature all have to be satisfied at once, and they conflict.

## Research topics

- Sensitivity analysis of design variables using design of experiments (DOE) and response surface methods
- Kriging and neural-network surrogates to cut FEA computation time
- Pareto-optimal solutions obtained with genetic algorithms
- Robust design accounting for manufacturing tolerances

## Outcome

Thousands of FEA runs can be replaced within a few hours, allowing a much wider candidate set to be
screened at the early design stage.
