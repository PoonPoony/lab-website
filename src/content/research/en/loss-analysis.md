Accurate efficiency prediction requires losses to be resolved component by component.
We compute loss components using finite element analysis (FEA) and verify them experimentally.

## Research topics

- Separation models for hysteresis, eddy-current and excess losses
- Influence of PWM carrier harmonics on core loss
- Magnet segmentation to suppress eddy-current loss in permanent magnets
- Local loss evaluation at the rotor surface and bridge regions

## Validation

No-load and load test results from manufactured prototypes are compared with simulation to
calibrate the loss coefficients and to derive efficiency maps over the full operating range.
