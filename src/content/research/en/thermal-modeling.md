Continuous output power is ultimately limited by temperature. Extracting maximum performance
without exceeding winding insulation class or magnet demagnetization limits requires accurate
thermal prediction.

## Research topics

- Transient and steady-state temperature prediction based on lumped-parameter thermal networks (LPTN)
- Identification of contact thermal resistance and equivalent end-winding conductivity
- Comparison of water-jacket and oil-spray cooling arrangements
- Electromagnetic–thermal coupled analysis to determine operating limits

## Experiments

Temperature-rise tests on prototypes instrumented with embedded thermocouples are used to
inversely identify and calibrate the model parameters.
