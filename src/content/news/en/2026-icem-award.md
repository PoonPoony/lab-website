Ph.D. candidate Minsu Kim received a Best Paper Award at the International Conference on
Electrical Machines (ICEM 2026), held in Athens, Greece.

The awarded paper reported a 38% reduction in IPMSM torque ripple through an asymmetric
flux-barrier design, and was recognized for presenting both design guidelines and
experimental verification on a prototype.

Congratulations!
