Our laboratory is recruiting new graduate students (M.S., Ph.D., and integrated M.S./Ph.D.)
for the Spring 2027 semester.

## Research areas

- Electric machine design and electromagnetic analysis (IPMSM, SynRM, etc.)
- Loss analysis and efficiency mapping
- Thermal modeling and cooling design
- Optimal design and machine-learning applications

## Who can apply

Students from electrical, mechanical or automotive engineering backgrounds are all welcome.
Experience with commercial FEA tools or programming is helpful but not required.

## Contact

Please send a short introduction and your transcript to the PI by email.
Lab visits and informal chats are always welcome.
