This paper addresses the lumped parameter thermal network(LPTN) analysis method for temperature prediction of an interior

permanent magnet synchronous motor (IPMSM). As the systems incorporating motors demand higher output power and torque, the

need for motor downsizing continues to grow. However, the reduction in surface area for dissipating the losses generated within the motor

has led to persistent thermal issues. Consequently, the importance of accurate temperature analysis for motors has become increasingly

significant. In the proposed LPTN analysis, the accurate prediction of motor temperature heavily depends on the estimation of the

convective heat transfer coefficient and thermal contact resistance coefficient. To address this, the paper presents a method that employs

particle swarm optimization, a global optimization algorithm, to minimize the error between experimental results and analytical

predictions.
