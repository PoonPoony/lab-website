This paper deals with the electromagnetic force according to the number of poles and slots for ultra-high-speed permanent magnet synchronous motors. To ensure mechanical stability by vibration, the numbers of poles and slots were determined by considering the maximum speed, and the rotational stability affected by the vibration of the rotor.
