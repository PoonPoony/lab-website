This paper proposes a 2-dimensional (2D) equivalent magnetic circuit (EMC) considering the axial leakage of a concentrated fluxtype synchronous motor (CFSM). The CFSM is a typical motor using non-rare-earth magnets and is used in various industries. However, in designing a CFSM, 3-dimensional (3D) finite element analysis (FEA) is required to consider axial leakage. The 3D FEA takes a long time to solve and is inconvenient for pre-processing. Therefore, a 2D EMC that is faster and does not require pre-processing is suitable for performing the optimum design of CFSM. In this paper, the 2D EMC of CFSM is verified by comparing EMC and FEA results.
