This paper investigates the impact of dual-phase magnetic material (DPMM) application on the energy consumption characteristics of multilayer interior permanent magnet synchronous motors (ML IPMSMs) for electric vehicle traction under various driving cycles. ML IPMSMs with and without DPMM are analyzed and compared in terms of losses and efficiency using finite element analysis. Efficiency maps are constructed considering AC copper loss, and iron loss over the entire operating range. A longitudinal vehicle model is then employed to evaluate driving-cycle-based energy consumption using the derived efficiency maps. Representative urban and highway driving cycles are considered to reflect different operating conditions. In addition, the validity of the analysis framework is indirectly verified by comparing analytical and experimental results for the non-DPMM model. The results indicate that the effect of DPMM application on energy consumption depends on driving cycle characteristics.
