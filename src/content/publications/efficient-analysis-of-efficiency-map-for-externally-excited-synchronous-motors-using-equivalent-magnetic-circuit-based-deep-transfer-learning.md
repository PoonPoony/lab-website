Externally excited synchronous motors (EESMs) have high degree-of-freedom in controlling the current vector for armature winding, field current, and rotational speed, so that EESMs can be operated in wide torque and speed range. In general, 2-D finite element analysis (FEA) is used for analysis of efficiency map for EESMs because of its high accuracy. Therefore, a lot of 2-D FEA should be performed repeatedly according to the current vector, field current, and rotational speed to calculate the flux linkage and iron loss of the EESMs. This leads to increased costs in designing high-efficiency EESMs, so the development of an analysis technique for efficient efficiency map is necessary. This paper proposes an equivalent magnetic circuit (EMC) based deep transfer learning for efficient analysis of efficiency map for EESMs. EMC allows us to calculate flux-linkage and iron loss of EESMs rapidly, but correction method should be accompanied because of its relatively low accuracy compared to 2-D FEA. The proposed method incorporates a large amount of EMC-based flux-linkage and iron loss, and a small amount of 2-D FEA-based flux-linkage and iron loss so that the efficiency map for EESM can be predicted accurately with reduced computation cost. The proposed method is applied to analysis of the efficiency map, and its validity is verified through simulation and experimental results.
