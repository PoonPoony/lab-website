This paper proposes an extended sizing method for wound field synchronous motors (WFSMs) that enables simultaneous evaluation of electrical and thermal characteristics during the preliminary design stage. Conventional sizing approaches developed for permanent magnet synchronous motors (PMSMs) have limitations when applied to WFSMs due to excitation-dependent flux generation and magnetic nonlinearity. To address this issue, a two-dimensional equivalent magnetic circuit (2D EMC) is incorporated to improve the accuracy of electrical parameter estimation by reflecting magnetic saturation effects. Furthermore, a lumped-parameter thermal equivalent circuit is integrated into the sizing process to evaluate temperature rise based on calculated losses. Since both electrical and thermal parameters are governed by motor geometry, the proposed method systematically links size-related variables—shape ratio (SR), torque per rotor volume (TRV), and torque density (TD)—with electromagnetic performance and thermal behavior. Using the proposed electro-thermal sizing method, an improved WFSM design satisfying both performance and thermal constraints is obtained. The proposed method enables a balanced design process by simultaneously considering electrical performance, mechanical characteristics, and thermal behavior during the preliminary design stage. As a result, the reliability and practicality of preliminary WFSM design can be improved through fast and accurate electro-thermal performance prediction.
