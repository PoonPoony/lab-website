This paper proposes a method of improving the performance of multilayer interior permanent magnet synchronous motor (ML IPMSM) by applying an electric transmission using a winding changeover. The ML IPMSM can compensate for a low magnetic torque by maximizing the reluctance torque but has a problem with a low power factor in the high-speed region. Consequently, as the rotational speed increases, the power of the ML IPMSM decreases. To address this problem, this paper adjusts the number of series turns of an armature winding. Reducing the number of series turns improves the power factor and increases the region satisfying maximum power. Therefore, the operating mode of ML IPMSM with the electric transmission is divided into two modes according to the operating point; one is the large number of series turns, and the other is the small number of series turns. This paper examines the electrical performance of the two modes and introduces a method for determining the optimal mode selection based on the operating point. Before introducing those, the disadvantage of the ML IPMSM using ferrite PM and a concept of the electric transmission are explained in detail. Finally, to verify the proposed method, the test and finite element analysis results are compared.
